Information we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, mobile number and, if you choose to share it, your email address, when you submit an enquiry form on this website.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry about Mizu & Ki by call, SMS, WhatsApp or email, as you agreed when submitting the form.

  • To understand which advertisements and pages lead to enquiries, and to improve them.
  • To prevent spam and misuse of the enquiry forms.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

  • The developer's sales team (Assetz Property Group) and our own sales staff, so that your enquiry can be handled.

  • Authorities, where we are required to by law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and to follow up on your interest in the project, and afterwards only for as long as required for legal, tax or record-keeping purposes.

Your choices and rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, to correct it, or to erase it, and you can withdraw your consent to being contacted at any time. To do any of these, or to stop receiving calls and messages from us, use the contact details below. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings.
